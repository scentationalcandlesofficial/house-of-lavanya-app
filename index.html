How this works: weigh the empty container first — that's the Tare Weight (enter 0 if there's no separate container, e.g. tea lights or wax shapes) — then weigh it again once poured to get the Gross Weight. The calculator works out how much wax you actually used and suggests a wholesale & retail price using a cost-based, industry-standard formula — wholesale ≈ 2× total cost, retail ≈ 3.5× total cost by default (both fully adjustable below). How this works: two separate things live here. Wholesale Pricing generates its own price list (PDF + a password-gated web page) for distributors — completely separate from your retail catalog and never touched by the regular "Update Catalog" button. Festival Sale applies a temporary discount to your public retail Digital Catalog and, while active, also produces a separate dated PDF snapshot — both only refresh when you click Update Catalog.

Wholesale Pricing

Set a default wholesale rate as a percentage of your retail price, with optional overrides per category or per candle. Generating the wholesale catalog is a separate, on-demand step — change the rate and regenerate any time, e.g. to prepare a different rate for a different distributor.

Distributors enter this once on the wholesale web page (remembered on their device after that). Not strong security — just keeps casual visitors and search engines out.

Festival Sale OFF

Discounts your public retail Digital Catalog — shows the original price struck through next to the sale price, with a banner. Also generates a separate (Sale) PDF alongside your regular Premium PDF (which is never changed). Takes effect the next time you click Update Catalog at the top of the page, and stays on until you switch it off here and update again.
